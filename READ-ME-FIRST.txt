ONE THING WORTH CONSIDERING (not included)
  The Build Wall still sits between Read and Buy, and right now it renders its
  empty state — no builds yet. That is 788px of "nothing here yet" on a phone
  before anyone reaches the cover. Moving Buy above Builds would put the cover
  at about 3.2 screens instead of 4.1.

  I left it alone because it is a judgment call, not a bug: the wall in front of
  the cover is how you signal that this is a project people take part in, not
  just a book for sale. Once you have real builds up there it earns the position.
  Say the word and it is another two-line change.
