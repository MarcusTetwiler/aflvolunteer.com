1. THE CUT-OFF HEAD IN THE ARTICLE 5 IMAGE
   Fixed, and the cause was structural rather than a bad number.

   The art is portrait 2:3. Covering a wide, viewport-height band showed only
   about 42% of its height — and because the crop window RESIZES with the
   viewport, any fixed percentage anchor slides across the composition and
   bisects a different figure at a different screen size. I measured it:

        1440x900  clean
        1440x800  cuts the seated operator
        1440x1000 cuts the lower pointing figure
        1920x900  cuts the seated operator

   No single percentage can work. So wide screens now get a purpose-made crop
   whose subjects all sit in the TOP HALF, anchored to the top — trimming then
   only ever happens at the bottom, below everyone's chin. Verified clean from
   1024x700 through 2560x1200. Narrow screens keep the portrait original, which
   barely crops there.

   The operator now appears complete: head, headset, controller.

2. ELENA / ANDREW PARITY
   Andrew's panel had no filter bar, no legend and no cartography footer, which
   is exactly why it felt unfinished next to Elena's. All three added — but in
   his own vocabulary rather than as copies of hers:

     Filter bar:  CONTRACTS — Broadcast | Private
                  (his data already carries this distinction; it filters for

   I also fixed two things that surfaced while building it: the filter bar was

   dim to read at 10px on near-black.
