# After import: author review checklist

1. At landing, verify approved hero still image, legible text and Enter the Grid. No video should load.
2. Build two contrasting machines through all six stages. Confirm silhouette/module changes and opposite trade-offs. Test the skeletal/large-pack compatibility message. Fabricate and inspect.
3. Choose callsign/country, continue without email, train or skip training, enter grid, race with actual thumb/keyboard control. Verify misses, recovery, reserve and retries.
4. Compare a clean race and deliberate poor/idle race. Second place must be earned. Guided practice must remain unranked even after guidance is turned off.
5. Verify leaderboard → Talon → quiet closure. Qualified result gets offer only after the sporting profile; other finishes go to book without invented podium.
6. Accept: map overview → contested geography → anonymous private records → wide map. Continue is always available. Decline: only a glimpse. Both reveal original cover/sample.
7. Refresh at an unanswered offer and after accept/decline. Confirm no automatic decision or repeated forced offer.
8. Save/name/duplicate/tune/restore two hangar aircraft. Confirm old results stay on old revisions. Check local-only storage messaging.
9. Open current sample without email. Launch list is separate. No purchase is active without a retailer link.
10. On real iPhone: portrait/landscape, browser bars, safe areas, two-thumb controls, text zoom, reduced motion, keyboard/screen-reader alternatives, scroll/focus and responsiveness.

GitHub import is not the same as public-launch acceptance. Commit to main can trigger Vercel automatically. Existing optional email services require their existing environment variables. Do not claim live signup or verified friend challenges without separate checks.
